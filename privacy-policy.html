Privacy Policy

How information may be collected and used when you visit AroundByte.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AroundByte ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with this website. It applies to information collected through this website and describes general practices that may also be relevant to our products. Individual products may provide additional privacy information where needed.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, and any details you provide. We use it to respond to your enquiry and maintain relevant correspondence.

Information collected automatically

When you visit a website, technical information may be processed by the hosting provider or security services. This can include IP address, browser type, device information, pages requested, referring pages, and timestamps. Such information may be used to deliver the website, protect it, diagnose issues, and understand basic site performance.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website or third-party services used on it may use cookies or similar technologies for essential operation, preferences, measurement, or advertising. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. More information is available in our Cookie Policy.

Advertising

We may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements if and when those services are enabled. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads, subject to their own policies and applicable choices available to users. Google's information about how it uses data from sites that use its services is available in Google's published privacy resources.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and protect the website.
  • To respond to messages and enquiries.
  • To understand website performance and improve content.
  • To support advertising, where advertising services are enabled.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the website, deliver hosting or security, provide analytics, or serve advertisements. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect rights, users, and the website.

Data retention and security

We retain correspondence and technical information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above or as required by law. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of certain personal information. You can also control cookies through your browser and use available advertising privacy controls. To make a privacy enquiry, contact us using the address below.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the matter.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or its services change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
