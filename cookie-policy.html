Cookie Policy

A straightforward explanation of cookies and related technologies.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how AroundByte and services used on this website may use cookies and similar technologies.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. They can help a website remember settings, support essential functions, understand usage, and deliver or measure advertising.

How cookies may be used

  • Essential functions: to support security, network delivery, and basic website operation.
  • Preferences: to remember choices where such features are available.
  • Measurement: to understand visits and improve website content, if analytics services are enabled.
  • Advertising: third-party advertising providers, including Google if enabled, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertisements.

Third-party cookies

Some technologies may be provided by third parties. Their use of information is governed by their own privacy and cookie policies. We do not control cookies set directly by third-party services.

Managing cookies

You can usually view, block, or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how certain websites or third-party features work. Where required, consent and advertising choices will be presented through the relevant controls.
